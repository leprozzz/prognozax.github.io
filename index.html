# Privacy Policy – PrognozaX **Last updated: October 29, 2026** ## 1. Introduction PrognozaX is an application developed by ProleApps that allows users to explore historical weather conditions for manually selected locations and dates, including historical data spanning up to 37 years. We respect your privacy. PrognozaX does not require user registration, does not collect personal information for identification, and does not use the device's GPS to determine your location. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information and how third-party weather services are used. ## 2. Information We Do Not Collect PrognozaX does not: - Require an account or registration. - Access or collect your device's GPS location. - Collect your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information. - Use advertising or tracking technologies. - Sell personal information to third parties. ## 3. Manually Entered Locations and Weather History Users manually enter or select a location and a date to retrieve historical weather information. The application stores the selected locations and weather history locally on the user's device. This allows users to revisit previously searched locations and access historical weather information without having to repeat the entire search. When the application is opened, it may retrieve additional historical weather data for previously selected locations to fill in missing days in the local weather history. The locally stored information may include: - Manually selected locations or geographic coordinates associated with those locations. - Selected dates and historical weather records. - Cached weather data used to improve application performance and continuity. This information is stored locally on the device and is not collected by ProleApps through a user account or personal data collection system. ## 4. Third-Party Weather Services PrognozaX uses weather data provided by Open-Meteo and its associated weather data sources, including ERA5 reanalysis data. To retrieve weather information, the application may send a weather query containing the manually selected location and relevant date range to the weather service. These requests are necessary for the application's core functionality. Open-Meteo and its infrastructure may process technical information associated with API requests, such as the requested location, date range, IP address, or other connection-related information, in accordance with their own privacy policies and service terms. PrognozaX does not control the data processing practices of these third-party services. For more information, please review: - Open-Meteo Privacy Policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms - Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/ ## 5. Data Storage and Retention Locations and historical weather information are stored locally within the application on the user's device. The application may update its local weather history when new data becomes available or when previously missing days need to be retrieved. ProleApps does not operate a user account system or a personal cloud database for PrognozaX. Locally stored application data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through the device's application settings, or otherwise removed by the operating system. Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data. ## 6. Data Sharing ProleApps does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information for advertising or marketing purposes. The application communicates with Open-Meteo to retrieve weather data based on user-selected locations and dates. This communication is necessary to provide the application's functionality. No personal user profile is created by PrognozaX. ## 7. Children's Privacy PrognozaX does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The application does not require users to provide personal information or create an account. ## 8. Security PrognozaX stores its application data locally on the user's device. We recommend that users keep their devices secure and use appropriate device-level security measures. Because the application does not operate a personal user account or cloud database, ProleApps does not maintain a separate server-side repository of users' saved locations or weather histories. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at the location where this Privacy Policy is made available. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically. ## 10.
